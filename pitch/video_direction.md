# Pitch video: direction (v2)

The team's notes from the Sunday review, for whoever edits the video next. The video itself is built in
`pitch/video/` (Remotion); the lines we speak over it are in `video_speaker_notes.md`.

## Shape

1. **Intro and team** stay as they are: logo build, then the eight of us and our five disciplines.
2. **The problem fills the first part**, before any product, so the audience feels why it matters:
   - **Open with the Brooklyn collapse** (1321 Flatbush Ave, November 11, 2026) as the starting point.
     Say what happened and that **the cause is under investigation**. Never say anything could have
     prevented it, and no blame. The human toll is said aloud, not put on screen.
   - Then the challenge: the warning signs are in public records, but each party (architect, engineer,
     geotech, contractor, DOB, neighbours) sees only their slice, and records about one site are filed on
     another. People work in silos.
   - What we're after is information during the process, shared by everyone: **one live, evolving
     checklist**, as risk intelligence for future projects. Not a claim to prevent failures.
   - **The cost today:** this kind of site research takes several thousand dollars and one to three weeks
     of lead time. That is the return on 4site. On screen it is labelled as our team's estimate from
     practice; there is no public source for it.
3. **The solution is the current product**, shown as a product video (not a live walkthrough), in the same
   style as v1 but re-recorded on the latest code. It covers every update: similar projects with lessons
   and "Watch out for", Strong, Some and Weak matches, setup that already knows the lot, the checklist as a
   compliance register with milestones and the trail behind each item, pre-filing items from DOB NOW
   objections, early warnings, and the chat (quick first answer, reads the project's own checklist).
4. Then the explanation scenes, data, stack (with **Vercel** for the web app and **Cityweft**), impact,
   limits and next steps, how we built it, and the outro line.

## Rules for the edit

- **Section headers never sit on top of the product.** A step title gets its own short card before the
  clip, or sits in empty space; it never covers the UI it describes.
- Don't use "in plain English".
- Every label must fit: the pie chart's slice reads "Engineer" in full.
- Light mode only, no captions, no voiceover. The pace leaves room to speak (about 2.5 words a second).
- The words to avoid stay the same: "predicts", "will fail", "safe", "would have prevented". No names of
  owners, applicants or contractors. Every number names its source.
- The DOB objection check is in the product now as pre-filing items (filings held at objections). What
  the objections were *about* is not public yet, so that part stays in next steps.
